# Basic Lore

The current lore is intentionally provisional.

The basic premise is:

The player dies in their original world.

Instead of experiencing a normal death, they become the target of a magical summoning ritual from another world.

The ritual fails.

Rather than being summoned correctly to its intended destination, the player is thrown into an unfamiliar magical world and begins an entirely new life there.

This premise exists primarily to justify:

character creation;
the player's lack of knowledge about the world;
unusual race possibilities;
magical affinity;
progression from almost nothing;
the player's freedom to become something entirely different from their previous life.

The exact cosmology, identity of the summoners, reason for the failed ritual, and relationship between worlds may change later.

Do not hardcode excessive lore assumptions into gameplay systems unless they become canonical.

See [character creation](../systems/character-creation.md) for the mechanical choices this premise supports.
