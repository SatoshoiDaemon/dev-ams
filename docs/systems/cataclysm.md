# Trial of Cataclysm

## Trial of Cataclysm

The Trial of Cataclysm is a post-game Boss Rush.

It becomes available after completing the main game.

The player may invoke it at any time after unlocking it.

Its purpose is to be deliberately brutal.

The Trial sequentially places the player against the major bosses from the campaign.

## Cataclysm Resource Persistence

Resources and character state persist between bosses.

Do NOT automatically fully restore the player after each fight.

Persistent state includes relevant things such as:

- HP
- Mana
- Tenacity
- consumables
- status state
- other finite resources

where appropriate.

The exact list may be adjusted by explicit Trial rules.

The central concept is:

Resource preservation across the entire Boss Rush matters.

The Trial should punish reckless consumption in early fights.
