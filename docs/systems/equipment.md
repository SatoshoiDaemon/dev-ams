# Equipment and Legendary Weapons

Combat-ready weapon capacity and the action costs of changing weapons or armor are specified in [combat](combat.md). Equipment construction is defined in [crafting](crafting.md); proposed equipment Sigils are documented in [Sigils](sigils.md).

## Armor Slots

The player has five primary armor/equipment slots:

- Helmet
- Gloves
- Chest
- Greaves
- Special Trinket

Greaves represents the combined lower-body slot, approximately including pants and boots.

The fifth slot is reserved for a more significant special equipment piece.

Examples might include:

- cloak
- mantle
- special artifact

The exact equipment categories may evolve.

## Trinkets and Talismans

Separate from the primary armor equipment, the player has:

5 Trinket / Talisman Slots

These items primarily provide passive effects.

Examples:

- attribute modifiers
- damage modifiers
- resource modifiers
- status modifiers
- conditional effects
- triggered passives
- build-specific bonuses

Trinkets should be strongly compatible with the Node/effect system rather than existing only as raw stat increases.

## Excalibur

Excalibur is intended to be:

The strongest pre-built sword in the game.

It has:

- unique Nodes
- exceptional properties
- endgame-level Scaling
- special identity

It should remain distinct from procedurally customized or player-created weapons.

The existence of Excalibur does not imply that every possible player-built weapon must necessarily be weaker in every possible context.

It is specifically the strongest major sword provided as a predefined finished weapon, barring other explicit legendary exceptions.

## Demon King's Sword

The Demon King's Sword is another exceptional legendary weapon.

It exists alongside Excalibur as an explicitly notable exception/peer in the highest tier of predefined weapons.

Its exact properties are defined elsewhere.

Do not normalize either sword into ordinary loot generation.
