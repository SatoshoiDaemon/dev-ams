# Equipment and Legendary Weapons

> **Specification status:** The canonical section defines slots, item budgets, level growth, weight classes, equipment actions, and the relationship between equipment, Sigils, attributes, and Status Effects.

Combat-ready weapon capacity and the action costs of changing weapons or armor are specified in [combat](combat.md). Equipment construction is defined in [crafting](crafting.md); proposed equipment Sigils are documented in [Sigils](sigils.md).

## Armor Slots

The player has five primary armor/equipment slots:

- Helmet
- Gloves
- Chest
- Greaves
- Special Trinket

Greaves represents the combined lower-body slot, approximately including pants and boots.

The fifth slot is reserved for a more significant special equipment piece.

Examples might include:

- cloak
- mantle
- special artifact

The exact equipment categories may evolve.

## Trinkets and Talismans

Separate from the primary armor equipment, the player has:

5 Trinket / Talisman Slots

These items primarily provide passive effects.

Examples:

- attribute modifiers
- damage modifiers
- resource modifiers
- status modifiers
- conditional effects
- triggered passives
- build-specific bonuses

Trinkets should be strongly compatible with the Node/effect system rather than existing only as raw stat increases.

## Excalibur

Excalibur is intended to be:

The strongest pre-built sword in the game.

It has:

- unique Nodes
- exceptional properties
- endgame-level Scaling
- special identity

It should remain distinct from procedurally customized or player-created weapons.

The existence of Excalibur does not imply that every possible player-built weapon must necessarily be weaker in every possible context.

It is specifically the strongest major sword provided as a predefined finished weapon, barring other explicit legendary exceptions.

## Demon King's Sword

The Demon King's Sword is another exceptional legendary weapon.

It exists alongside Excalibur as an explicitly notable exception/peer in the highest tier of predefined weapons.

Its exact properties are defined elsewhere.

Do not normalize either sword into ordinary loot generation.

## Canonical Quantified Equipment Contract

Equipment slots are: 1 combat-ready weapon, 1 off-hand or shield, Helmet, Gloves, Chest, Greaves, Special Trinket, and 5 Trinket/Talisman slots. A character may own unlimited non-equipped items unless a category-specific inventory rule applies. Swapping the combat-ready weapon costs `50 Action Points`; changing armor costs `100 Action Points`; swapping weapon and off-hand together costs `100`.

Each item has `item_level 1..7000`, `weight`, `defense`, `base_damage`, `scaling`, `attribute_requirements`, `node_capacity`, and `sigil_capacity`. Item level contribution is `floor(item_level / 10)` budget points. Standard item budgets are: one-hand light `20 + 2 × level_tier`, two-hand `30 + 3 × level_tier`, shield `25 + 2 × level_tier`, armor piece `15 + 2 × level_tier`, catalyst `20 + 2 × level_tier`, trinket `10 + level_tier`, where `level_tier = clamp(floor(item_level / 100), 0, 70)`. A budget point buys `1 base damage`, `1 defense`, `1 attribute point`, or `1 Node/Sigil Weight`; the item definition must record the allocation.

Weight classes are Very Light `1..10`, Light `11..20`, Normal `21..35`, Heavy `36..55`, and Very Heavy `56+`. Total equipped Weight above `100` reduces Priority by `floor((weight - 100) / 2)` and above `150` prevents sprint/movement actions; there is no hidden carry rule. Armor Defense is `2 × allocated defense`; weapon Base Damage is `3 × allocated damage`; catalysts use `2 × allocated damage` as magical Base Damage.

Requirements are hard validation: a missing attribute requirement prevents equip and logs the missing ID/value. Attribute bonuses apply before derived HP/Tenacity/Mana recalculation. Status-producing equipment uses the StatusPacket contract in [Status Effects](effects.md); it may apply existing IDs or registered Glyph-native IDs, and repeated same-effect applications combine before competition.

Standard equipment has `0..5` Sigil slots by template: one-hand light 2, two-hand 3, shield 3, armor piece 1, catalyst 4, legendary items 5. Excalibur and Demon King's Sword are level 7000 unique items with budget `250`, 5 Sigil slots, and fixed stable IDs; they are not generated by ordinary crafting. Tests must cover slot counts, action costs, weight thresholds, budget arithmetic, requirement failure, and status-source ownership.
