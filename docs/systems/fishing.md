# Fishing

## Fishing

Fishing requires a fishing rod.

Fishing rods have progression tiers or levels.

The player's first fishing rod should normally be obtainable from a fisherman NPC at the docks of Tidal Town.

Fishing works as a timing activity.

Basic sequence:

```text
Start fishing
↓
Wait
↓
Fish bites
↓
Player receives reaction window
↓
Player presses the appropriate input
↓
Catch succeeds or fails
```

The terminal interface should clearly communicate the timing state.

## Fishing Rewards

Fishing may produce:

- fish
- rare fish
- materials
- special objects
- treasure chests

Some treasure chests can ONLY be obtained through fishing.

Fishing-exclusive treasure may contain valuable progression objects such as:

- weapon templates
- equipment templates
- rare materials
- special Nodes

Different locations should support different fishing tables.

Fishing rod quality may influence:

- available fish
- rare catches
- reaction window
- treasure chance
- location accessibility
