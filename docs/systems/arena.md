# The Arena

## The Arena

The Arena is a dungeon/activity located in Tidal Town.

It can be entered from the beginning of the game.

The player can use its earliest fights as part of early progression.

However, Arena difficulty increases dramatically over time.

The Arena contains:

100 opponents

that the player must defeat progressively.

## Arena Rewards

Arena victories provide unusually valuable combat progression.

Rewards may include:

- unique melee techniques
- increased XP compared to ordinary fights
- equipment
- special Equipment Nodes
- unique rewards

The Arena is therefore simultaneously:

- challenge content
- progression content
- martial build content

## Arena Magic Restriction

Magic cannot be used inside The Arena.

This is a core Arena rule.

The purpose is to create a progression path that strongly tests:

- weapons
- physical combat
- Fighting Styles
- equipment
- martial builds

rather than allowing every encounter to be solved through the player's normal spell kit.

The restriction should be represented through dungeon/ruleset configuration rather than a hardcoded check scattered through combat code.
