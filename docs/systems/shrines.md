# Knight Shrines and The Kings

## The Shrines

The Shrines are dungeon content dedicated primarily to obtaining advanced magic.

There are multiple Shrine categories.

Current known categories:

- Knight Shrines
- The Kings

Shrines are not ordinary spell shops.

Their magic is earned through dedicated challenges.

## Knight Shrines

Knight Shrines are dungeons dedicated to legendary knights from the kingdom's past.

Each Shrine culminates in a fight against:

the ghost/spirit of the corresponding Knight

Defeating that Knight grants their signature magic.

These rewards are effectively:

Epic Spells

They are stronger and more unique than ordinary magic.

They also contain unique Nodes unavailable through normal spell progression.

## Knight Shrine Distribution

There are intended to be:

3 Knight Shrines per Element

Each corresponds to a distinct Knight/signature magical reward.

This creates multiple advanced spell paths for every supported element.

The exact Knight identities and spell designs are defined separately.

## The Kings

The Kings are Shrines dedicated to ancient kings, sovereigns, and rulers of the continent.

They are significantly harder than ordinary Knight Shrines.

Their rewards include:

- extremely rare Nodes
- extremely powerful Nodes
- highly unique mechanics

These should represent some of the strongest build-defining progression in the game.

The Kings should not merely drop numerically larger versions of standard Nodes.

Their rewards should enable unusual mechanics or interactions.
